Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We want every client to feel confident investing in our services. This policy sets out when refunds are available.

Website build (setup fee)

If work has not yet commenced on your project, you are entitled to a full refund of your setup fee. Once the strategy session has taken place or design work has begun, the setup fee is non-refundable as significant time and expertise has been invested.

Annual management fee

Annual management fees are non-refundable once the renewal period has begun. If you wish to cancel ongoing management, please give us 30 days notice and we will not charge for the following renewal period.

Sales commission

Commission is only charged on sales we successfully close on your behalf. There is no upfront fee and therefore no refund applicable.

How to request a refund

To request a refund or discuss your situation, please email us at info@curatedwebstudios.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Get in touch

Tell us a little about you and your work. We reply personally, usually within one working day.
