What’s Registry?

Curated Registry is our checkout, built for art. Each work is catalogued, certified and ready to sell from a single record, so a collector buys direct, ownership transfers, and the provenance updates itself. You don’t run a shop or manage a payment system; Registry is the transaction layer sitting quietly behind your site. It’s the best default for most artists, never mandatory.
