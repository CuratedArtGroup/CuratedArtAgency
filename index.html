Most web designers know how to make websites look good.

We know how to make them work.

With 16 years in sales and e-commerce, including helping generate over £4 million in sales and supporting 50+ creative businesses and artists, every website is built around one simple principle:

Trust comes before transactions.

We build websites that create trust, encourage conversations and turn interest into genuine collector enquiries.

Collectors don't buy original artwork the same way they buy everyday products. Before they commit, they want confidence. They want to understand the artist, connect with the work and know they're buying from someone they can trust.

That's why every Curated website is designed around the way art is actually bought. When direct online checkout is the right fit, we integrate it seamlessly, without making your website feel like just another online store.

FAQ
How long does a build take?

As quick as a week from kickoff. Most builds take one to two weeks depending on how quickly content and feedback come together.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. The site is handed straight over to you and it's yours. We walk you through everything at the launch session, and if you'd rather not touch it, Hosting & Care makes your updates for you.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Your site lives on your own domain and you own it. We help you connect it at launch if it's not set up yet.

Do you write the copy?

Yes. We shape the copy with you at kickoff, written in your voice and built around how your audience buys, then refine it together during the build.

Do I need Curated OPUS to sell?

No. OPUS comes free in year one with the Full Build, but it's optional: your site can sell through your own payment links, or work purely through enquiries. It can work with the One Page Portfolio too, but the free first year is a Full Build perk specifically.

Do you only build for artists?

Art is where we come from and what we know best, but the approach travels. We also design and build for galleries and wider creative businesses, quoted after a free call. Tell us what you do and how you sell, and we'll tell you honestly if we're the right fit.
