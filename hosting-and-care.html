FAQ
Do I need Hosting & Care?

No. Your site is handed to you fully yours at launch. Hosting & Care is for anyone who'd rather not manage it themselves.

Swapping artworks, updating prices or availability, small copy changes and the like. For a bigger redesign, we'll scope and quote separately.

How fast are changes made?

Send us what's changed and it's live the same week, usually sooner.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There's no lock-in, and your site stays fully yours whether you keep Hosting & Care or not.

Yes, they run well together. Hosting & Care keeps your site current, and Curated OPUS keeps every work sale-ready.
